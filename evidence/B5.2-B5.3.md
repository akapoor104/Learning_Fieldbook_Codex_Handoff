# B5.2 and B5.3 · Relative preview, conversation and revocation

The starting revision was `99ede24e542b9832be2b54e5be24556e9912baf7` on `main`. That revision supplied the B5.1 invitation, revision pinning and download routes. The family and guest browser flows, conversations, teacher feedback and the revocation case were pending.

## T5 observations

- The learner and guardian opened the same preview for the named relative. It showed the selected words and practice photo. The guardian created the invitation using the keyboard. A second signed-in guest received 404 when redeeming the code or reading the conversation.
- The named guest signed in with their own virtual passkey, accepted the invitation, downloaded the photo, and posted, withdrew and restored a dated comment under their own name. The guardian could read it. The guest did not see a family-only comment, the teacher’s PDF, another entry or a private diary original. The learner could download that diary original.
- Adding a second photo and revising the source left the original guest selection intact. The family recorded the PDF with its writer and uploader and saved a separate paraphrase with its writer and recorder.
- Withdrawing the grant returned 404 on share detail, photo GET and HEAD, ranged GET, conversation read, comment write and replay, comment withdrawal and restore, and invitation reuse. Visiting the saved share URL showed the unavailable state and an empty visible list. The guardian still downloaded the original JPEG, which matched the published fixture SHA-256. The family archive still displayed the relative’s comment.
- The expired invitation returned 404 on repeated acceptance and download attempts, exposed no filename, and created no visible grant. The browser showed an unavailable invitation. The test advances only the synthetic invitation’s stored expiry to exercise this path.
