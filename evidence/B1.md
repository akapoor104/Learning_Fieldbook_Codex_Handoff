The login uses one credential per actor and PostgreSQL-backed sessions. A shared computer has a 15-minute idle window, a warning in the final minute, and an eight-hour absolute limit. The guardian’s independent verification lasts five minutes. Capture in this slice is a transient sentence editor. B2 carries journal saving and B6 carries creating projects.

## Remaining device check

The local environment had no connected Android device, Android SDK emulator or accelerator. There was also no physical user-verifying authenticator attached to the shared-computer rehearsal. The new GitHub browser job has a local-equivalent run recorded here. Its hosted execution is pending.

On a private rehearsal HTTPS origin using a disposable household, create distinct learner and two guardian setup codes with the [Home operator procedure](../deploy/home/README.md). Enroll the learner on the shared computer with their device credential or security key. Enroll a guardian on the Android device using its credential manager. Sign in from both devices, verify the correct Home and perform **Verify it’s you** on Android. On the shared computer, switch accounts and use Back after typing a fictional sentence. Record OS and browser versions, authenticator type, visible actor and expiry outcome. Keep setup codes out of the receipt. These device results are needed for a complete T1 receipt.
