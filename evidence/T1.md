# T1 device rehearsal

**Status pending.** The automated shared-browser and second-household checks passed on the merged B1 build. A parent’s passkey sign-in and reauthentication still need an Android device or Android OS emulator with a working credential manager. A physical shared-computer session still needs a participant with a local device credential or user-verifying security key.
