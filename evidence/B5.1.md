## Observations

- An ordinary guardian approved the fixture highlight and its attached JPEG for one named guest. Missing scribed-text confirmation gave 422, a stale revision gave 409, a missing CSRF token gave 403, and an out-of-household guest or a diary asset in the selection gave 404. Retrying the pending approval with the same ID and selection returned the original invitation. Explicit withdrawal of learner confirmation canceled a pending text invitation. Reconfirming the text did not reactivate that invitation.
- A second guest in the same household could not redeem the invitation. Two simultaneous redemptions by the named guest returned 204 and 404. The read returned the first revision's words with the scribed voice, learner, recorder and uploader. Editing the entry and adding another photo left the approved text and file selection intact. The JPEG matched the fixture bytes. HEAD returned 200 and a byte-range request returned 206.
- The other same-household guest and the second household received 404 for the share and photo. The selected guest received 404 for the unselected teacher PDF, later photo, generic family routes and a diary original that the learner could open. An expired invitation returned 404 and exposed no filename. An expired accepted share also returned 404.
- Unmatching and rematching an independently selected photo preserved the household attachment and removed it from its previous share. Revoking the primary share blocked a still-accessible original on GET, HEAD and range requests. After a server restart, the share and original still returned 404. Retrying the invitation failed, and the household downloaded the original bytes successfully.
